Peanuts* — The Classic Comic Strip

**Peanuts** is a timeless comic strip created by *Charles M. Schulz* in **1950**. It captures the joys and struggles of childhood, friendship, and imagination — all told through simple yet meaningful humor.

---

##  Main Characters

* **Charlie Brown** – A kind but unlucky boy who never gives up.
* **Snoopy** – His loyal beagle with a big imagination.
* **Lucy van Pelt** – Bold and bossy, offers “psychiatric help” for 5¢.
* **Linus van Pelt** – Thoughtful and always holding his blanket.
* **Woodstock** – Snoopy’s tiny yellow bird friend.

---

## About Snoopy

> “My life has no purpose, no direction, no aim, no meaning, and yet I’m happy. I can’t figure it out. What am I doing right?”
> — *Snoopy*

Snoopy isn’t your ordinary dog. He:

1. Imagines himself as a *World War I Flying Ace*. 
2. Writes novels on top of his red doghouse.
3. Loves root beer and dancing.

---

## Themes of *Peanuts*

| Theme                 | Description                                          |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Friendship**        | The bond between Charlie Brown and his friends.      |
| **Perseverance**      | Even when he fails, Charlie Brown keeps trying.      |
| **Imagination**       | Snoopy’s daydreams make ordinary life extraordinary. |
| **Humor with Wisdom** | Simple jokes that reveal deep truths.                |

---

## Why It’s Still Loved

* The art is simple yet expressive.
* The humor is heartwarming and relatable.
* It teaches optimism, resilience, and kindness.
* It connects with both children and adults.
